The leak · 2019

The censorship project had a name: Machine Learning Fairness

Google didn't delete the internet. It rank-ordered it — and put an AI system called Machine Learning Fairness in charge of the ordering, quietly, across Search, YouTube and News. Demote something far enough and it may as well not exist.

What the documents show

An AI system applied to ranking. Internal blacklists. Secret page-rank scores. A stated plan to force a "single point of truth" onto news organisations.

On 14 August 2019 I delivered the documents to the Department of Justice anti-trust division and published them through Project Veritas.

Read the documents →

Why it matters

Research on search ranking shows biased results can shift the voting preference of undecided voters from a 50–50 split to as far as 10–90, in either direction.

Whoever controls the ranking of information for the general public rules the world. The premise of this website

My Texas Senate testimony produced a unanimous, bipartisan vote empowering the State Attorney to issue subpoenas against Google and other Big Tech companies. That's the whole aim here: expose how the ranking works, so it can't be seized quietly.
